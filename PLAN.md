# THE SINAI TRAIL — Design Plan

*An Oregon Trail–style browser game of the 40-year journey from the far shore of the Red Sea to the fall of Jericho.*
*Teaching sources: BEMA Discipleship (Marty Solomon), David Pawson "Unlocking the Old Testament", BibleProject.*

---

## 1. Vision

The player leads one Israelite household inside the great camp. They must keep five people alive across ~550 miles of Sinai, Negev, Arabah and Moab while the whole nation is tested, marries YHWH at Sinai, fails at Kadesh, wanders, and finally crosses the Jordan. The game plays and *looks* exactly like the 1985 Apple II Oregon Trail (green phosphor text, chunky pixel scenes, "Press ENTER to size up the situation"), but every mechanic teaches the biblical text:

* **Greed** (hoarding manna, keeping Egyptian gold from the tabernacle, pocketing Jericho's devoted things) rots, costs trust, or kills.
* **Idolatry** (the golden calf, Baal Peor) kills and breaks the covenant mid-wedding.
* **Grumbling / demanding** (Massah, Taberah, Kibroth-hattaavah, the spies) is tracked — "You have tested me ten times" (Num 14:22) — and the tenth grumble triggers the sentence of the wilderness.
* **Trust and obedience** (the weak drink first at Marah, keep Sabbath, speak to the rock, march silently around Jericho) restore health, water, and *Emunah* (trust).

The desert is the classroom: "God had to get Egypt out of them… the desert is the refining fire" (BEMA 20).

## 2. Oregon Trail → Sinai Trail mapping

| Oregon Trail | Sinai Trail |

## 4. Systems

**Resources.** Water (days), Provisions (lbs — until manna), Manna (daily after Sin; obeys the gather rule), Flock (head), Donkeys, Silver (shekels), Egyptian Gold (jewelry — the idolatry/tabernacle fork), Spare skins, Sandals, Tent.

**People.** Five household members with health 0–100; illnesses; deaths. At Kadesh failure the game time-skips: everyone 20+ in year 2 dies over 38 years (unless the player sided with Caleb & Joshua — then the *player* survives as Caleb did, but the nation's sentence still stands).

**Emunah (trust) 0–100** — rises with obedient choices, falls with grumbling. Modulates event odds and the score. **Grumble counter** — 10 = "you have tested me ten times."

**Flags** — kept_gold, calf, hoarded, sabbath_broken, korah, struck_rock, looked_at_serpent, peor, achan, etc. Flags feed endings and the epitaphs.

**Calendar** — Hebrew months counted from the Exodus (Aviv = month 1, year 1). Sabbath every 7th day.

**Random events** (weighted by region, trust, pace): sandstorm; flash flood in the wadi (camp on the rock or on the floor?); scorpion in a cistern; a rotem bush with shade for one; an acacia find (fuel/wood/fodder); ar'ar fruit temptation; lost in the wadi (+7 miles); Amalekite raiders at the rear; heat-stroke; thirst; quail; a Midianite caravan; a dispute in the camp (blame vs. confess); a companion falls ill; the cloud stays (rest days).

**Scroll of Insight.** Every stop carries 1–3 short teachings, each tagged with its source and episode (e.g. "BEMA 21 · With All Your Soul", "Pawson · Unlocking the OT part 13", "BibleProject · Israel Tests Yahweh"). Nothing is attributed to a teacher that the transcripts don't say.

## 5. Look, sound, and map

* **Look**: 560×384 "hi-res" canvas, black background, green (#33ff33) phosphor text with faint scanlines; white/orange/purple accents sparingly (Apple II hi-res palette). Pixel font (Press Start 2P for headers, VT323 for body; monospace fallback). All scenes drawn in code (no image files) — caravan walking animation (people, donkey, flock, pillar of cloud), mountains, palms, the sea, the mountain of God, the camp with the tabernacle, Jericho's walls.
* **Sound**: WebAudio chiptune — an original modal (Dorian) melody loop plus square-wave beeps; toggle with S. A `MUSIC_URL` constant in `js/audio.js` lets you drop in a royalty-free Pixabay track (`assets/music/*.mp3`) instead.
* **Map**: press **M** anywhere while travelling — a pixel map of Egypt/Sinai/Negev/Edom/Moab/Canaan with the route, every stop, and a blinking marker for the current position; visited stops light up and list what happened there.
