# Names in The Exodus Trail — and what they mean

Hebrew meanings are the usual scholarly glosses; where a name is Egyptian in origin or the etymology is disputed, that is noted. Chapter references are where the person appears in the text the game follows.

## Your household (the default names, js/data/core.js)

| Name | Meaning | In the text |
|---|---|---|
| **Elishama** (אֱלִישָׁמָע) | "My God has heard" | Chief of the tribe of Ephraim in the census (Num 1:10); grandfather of Joshua (1 Chr 7:26-27) |
| **Tirzah** (תִּרְצָה) | "She is my delight", "pleasantness" | One of the five daughters of Zelophehad who won the right to inherit (Num 26:33; 27:1-11) |
| **Hur** (חוּר) | "White", "noble" (from the word for fine white linen); some link it to "hollow/cave" | Held up Moses' hand with Aaron at Rephidim (Ex 17:10-12); grandfather of Bezalel who built the tabernacle (Ex 31:2) |
| **Noa** (נֹעָה) | "Movement", "motion" — not the same name as Noah (נֹחַ, "rest") | Another daughter of Zelophehad (Num 26:33) |
| **Ithamar** (אִיתָמָר) | "Island (or land) of palms" | Aaron's fourth and youngest son; survives Nadab and Abihu and serves as priest (Ex 6:23; Lev 10) |

| Name | Meaning | In the text |
|---|---|---|
| **Nun** (נוּן) | "Fish" (Aramaic/Hebrew); also "posterity, continuance" | Father of Joshua (Ex 33:11) — the fishing scenes wink at him |
| **Achsah** (עַכְסָה) | "Anklet", "ankle ornament" | Caleb's daughter, given to Othniel and given the springs she asked for (Josh 15:16-19) |
| **Othniel** (עָתְנִיאֵל) | "Lion of God" or "strength of God" | Caleb's nephew; takes Debir; first of the judges (Josh 15:17; Judg 3:9) |
| **Hoglah** (חָגְלָה) | "Partridge" | Daughter of Zelophehad (Num 26:33) |
| **Jair** (יָאִיר) | "He enlightens", "he will shine" | Son of Manasseh who took the villages of Gilead (Num 32:41) |
| **Milcah** (מִלְכָּה) | "Queen" | Daughter of Zelophehad (Num 26:33) |

## The people you meet on the trail

| Name | Meaning | In the text |
|---|---|---|
| **Moses** (מֹשֶׁה, Moshe) | Egyptian *mes/mose*, "born of, child of" (as in Thut-mose); Exodus hears the Hebrew *mashah*, "drawn out" — "I drew him out of the water" (Ex 2:10) | Exodus – Deuteronomy |
| **Aaron** (אַהֲרֹן, Aharon) | Uncertain; possibly Egyptian; traditional glosses "exalted", "mountain of strength", "bright" | Ex 4 onward; dies at Mount Hor (Num 20) |
| **Miriam** (מִרְיָם) | Possibly Egyptian *mry*, "beloved"; Hebrew hearers connected it to *mar*, "bitter" (the same root as Marah) and *meri*, "rebellion" | Sings at the sea (Ex 15:20); Hazeroth (Num 12); dies at Kadesh (Num 20:1) |
| **Joshua** (יְהוֹשֻׁעַ, Yehoshua) | "YHWH is salvation". Moses renamed him from **Hoshea** (הוֹשֵׁעַ, "salvation", "he saves") at the sending of the spies (Num 13:16). The Aramaic short form *Yeshua* is the name rendered "Jesus" in Greek | Rephidim (Ex 17:9); Sinai; the spies; the Jordan; Jericho |
| **Caleb** (כָּלֵב) | Literally "dog" (*kelev*) — a term of fierce loyalty; often read as *kol-lev*, "whole heart", which is why the text says he "followed the LORD wholeheartedly" (Num 14:24) | The spies (Num 13-14); Hebron (Josh 14) |
| **Jethro** (יִתְרוֹ, Yitro) | "His excellence", "his abundance". Also called **Reuel** (רְעוּאֵל), "friend of God" | Priest of Midian, Moses' father-in-law; Rephidim (Ex 18) |
| **Korah** (קֹרַח) | "Bald", "ice/hail" | Leads the rebellion of 250 (Num 16) |
| **Dathan** (דָּתָן) | Uncertain; perhaps "fountain" or "belonging to a decree/law" | Reubenite ally of Korah (Num 16:1) |
| **Abiram** (אֲבִירָם) | "My father is exalted" | Dathan's brother (Num 16:1) |
| **Eleazar** (אֶלְעָזָר) | "God has helped" | Aaron's third son; receives the high priest's robes on Mount Hor (Num 20:25-28) |
| **Phinehas** (פִּינְחָס) | Egyptian *Pa-nehasi*, "the Nubian", "the dark-skinned one"; a Hebrew folk reading is "mouth of brass" | Stops the plague at Baal Peor (Num 25:7-8) |
| **Nadab** (נָדָב) | "Generous", "willing", "noble" | Aaron's eldest son; dies offering unauthorised fire (Lev 10:1-2) |
| **Abihu** (אֲבִיהוּא) | "He (God) is my father" | Aaron's second son; dies with Nadab (Lev 10:1-2) |
| **Balaam** (בִּלְעָם) | Traditional glosses "not of the people" (*bal-am*) or "devourer/swallower" (*bala*) | Hired by Balak to curse Israel; blesses instead (Num 22-24) |
| **Balak** (בָּלָק) | "Devastator", "one who lays waste", "empty" | King of Moab (Num 22) |
| **Rahab** (רָחָב) | "Broad", "wide", "spacious" | Hides the two spies in Jericho (Josh 2); ancestor of David (Matt 1:5) |
| **Achan** (עָכָן) | Linked in the text to *achor*, "trouble" — "the Valley of Achor" is named for him (Josh 7:26); 1 Chr 2:7 spells him **Achar**, "troubler" | Takes the devoted things from Jericho (Josh 7) |
| **Sihon** (סִיחוֹן) | Uncertain; perhaps "warrior", "one who sweeps away" | Amorite king of Heshbon (Num 21:21-31) |
| **Og** (עוֹג) | Uncertain; perhaps "long-necked", "giant", or "round (cake)" | King of Bashan; the iron bed (Deut 3:11) |
| **Amalek** (עֲמָלֵק) | Uncertain; folk etymology "dweller in the valley" or "people who lick up" | Attacks the rear at Rephidim (Ex 17:8-16; Deut 25:17-19) |
| **Zelophehad** (צְלָפְחָד) | Uncertain; perhaps "shadow of fear" or "first rupture" | Father of five daughters and no sons (Num 26:33; 27:1-11) |
| **Pharaoh** | Egyptian *per-aa*, "great house" — a title, not a name | Exodus 1-15 |
| **David** (דָּוִד) | "Beloved" | Named in the slingshot and Rahab scenes (1 Sam 17; Matt 1) |
| **Elijah** (אֵלִיָּהוּ) | "My God is YHWH" | Named under the broom bush (1 Kgs 19:4-8) |
| **Jesus** (Ἰησοῦς, from Yeshua) | "YHWH saves" — the same name as Joshua | The Jordan scene (John 6:49-51; Matt 3) |
| **Nazareth** (נָצְרַת) | Probably from *netzer*, "branch, shoot" (cf. Isa 11:1) | The Jordan scene |

|---|---|

| **Manna** (מָן) | From *man hu*, "what is it?" (Ex 16:15) |
| **Marah** (מָרָה) | "Bitter" (Ex 15:23) |
| **Elim** (אֵילִם) | "Great trees", "terebinths/palms" |
| **Massah / Meribah** | "Testing" / "quarrelling" (Ex 17:7) |
| **Taberah** | "Burning" (Num 11:3) |
| **Kibroth-hattaavah** | "Graves of craving" (Num 11:34) |
| **Hazeroth** | "Enclosures", "courtyards" |
| **Kadesh** | "Holy", "consecrated" |
| **Hormah** | "Destruction", "devoted to destruction" (Num 14:45) |
| **Gilgal** | "Rolling", "circle" — "today I have rolled away the reproach of Egypt" (Josh 5:9) |
| **Jericho** | Probably "moon (city)" (*yareach*) or "fragrant" |

| **Ezion-geber** | "Backbone of a man/giant" — the port on the Gulf of Aqaba (Num 33:35) |

| **Shema** | "Hear, listen — and do" (Deut 6:4) |

| **Matzevot** | "Standing stones" (Josh 4) |
